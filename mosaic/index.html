Median, first tile to picture

Safer communities, one tile at a time.

A location ping is only a location. A face in a frame is only a face. On its own, no tile tells you anything about anyone.

Mosaic collects tiles from every enrolled endpoint, matches them as they arrive and places each one where it belongs. Enough tiles, in the right order, show the resident.

Every endpoint sees more than the person enrolled. Faces and voices in view are matched as incidental residents, so the picture reaches the whole neighbourhood, including residents who never enrolled.

These data are anonymous until they are useful. Our partners never handle tiles. They receive the picture.
